Privacy Policy

Last updated: [[DATE]]

1. Who we are

Geo Router ("we", "us") is operated by [[OPERATOR NAME / REGISTERED BUSINESS NAME]], based in [[CITY, STATE, NIGERIA]]. Our website is [[https://yourdomain.com]]. For privacy questions, contact us at [[privacy@yourdomain.com]].

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use Geo Router, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

2. What we collect

  • Your device location. If you allow it in your browser, we read your precise location. While the page is open, your browser keeps updating it so we can show your position on the map. We do not read your location unless you grant permission, and you can revoke it at any time in your browser settings.
  • The destination you type. This is the place name you enter to get a route.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our servers record your IP address, browser and device type, the pages or API paths requested, and timestamps.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. We set a security cookie (CSRF) that protects forms and requests on this site. We also save your settings (distance unit, fuel use, fuel price and currency symbol) in your browser's local storage so you don't have to re-enter them. These stay on your device and are not sent to us. Advertising partners may set their own cookies (see section 6).
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we keep your email address and message so we can reply.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data.

3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • Calculating your route and showing your position, using your location and destination. Basis: your consent (the browser location prompt).
  • Showing the name of the place you are in. Your coordinates are converted into a place name. Basis: your consent.
  • Keeping the site secure and working, including preventing abuse and fixing errors, using technical data. Basis: our legitimate interests.
  • Showing ads that help keep the service free. Basis: your consent where required, otherwise our legitimate interests.
  • Replying to your messages. Basis: our legitimate interests.

4. What we do not do with your location

We use your location to answer your request. [[We do not save your location or your searched destinations in a database, link them to an identity, or sell them.]] To speed things up, the server may briefly keep results of recent place lookups in temporary memory. These are rounded or generic lookups and are cleared when the server restarts. Location data is sent to our server in the body of a secure request, not in the page address, so it is not written into our access logs. [[Confirm this matches your final setup.]]

5. Who else receives data

To provide the service, some data goes to other organisations:

  • Geocoding (place search). The destination you type, and your coordinates when we look up your place name, are sent from our server to [[OpenStreetMap Nominatim / your geocoding provider]].
  • Routing. The start and end coordinates are sent to [[our own routing server / OSRM public service]] to calculate the route.
  • Map tiles. Your browser loads map images directly from [[OpenStreetMap tile servers / your tile provider]], which means that provider sees your IP address and the map area you view.
  • Content delivery and fonts. Your browser loads code libraries and fonts from third-party hosts ([[unpkg.com, cdn.tailwindcss.com, Google Fonts]]). These hosts see your IP address.
  • Advertising. Google AdSense, described below.
  • Hosting. Our website is hosted by [[HOSTING PROVIDER]].

We do not sell personal data. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors (© OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under the ODbL).

6. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more at aboutads.info. You can also learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required (for example, for visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before ad cookies are used. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some parts of the site may not work correctly.

7. How long we keep data

  • Your location and destination: not stored after your request is answered [[confirm]].
  • Server logs (IP address, timestamps, paths): [[30]] days, then deleted.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, then [[12]] months.

8. International transfers

Some of the providers above, including Google and content delivery networks, process data outside Nigeria. Our servers are located in [[COUNTRY/REGION]]. Where personal data leaves Nigeria, we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards, as the NDPA requires.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used (this policy);
  • ask whether we hold data about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected or data erased;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing for marketing;
  • receive your data in a portable format, where applicable;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To use these rights, email [[privacy@yourdomain.com]]. You can stop location sharing at any time through your browser's site settings. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled.

10. Security

We use HTTPS to encrypt data between your browser and our site and limit access to our servers. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify the NDPC and affected people as the law requires.

11. Children

Geo Router is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If changes are significant, we will tell you on the site.
